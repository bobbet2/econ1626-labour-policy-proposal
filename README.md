# econ1626-labour-policy-proposal

## Overview
This repository contains a policy proposal prepared for **ECON1626: The Economics of AI** at RMIT University. The proposal analyses how artificial intelligence is reshaping the Australian labour market and recommends a three-part policy package to manage displacement, support transition, and direct AI deployment toward worker-complementary outcomes.

## Repository Structure
- `proposal.md` — the full policy brief
- `README.md` — this navigation guide

## Proposal Summary
Addressed to the **Deputy Secretary, Department of Employment and Workplace Relations (DEWR)**, the proposal evaluates the impact of AI on employment, wages, equity, and industry structure, and recommends:

1. **National Upskilling/Reskilling Program** — government-funded retraining for workers at risk of AI substitution, delivered through TAFE and short-course providers.
2. **AI-Augmentation Incentive Program** — a tax incentive directing firms to deploy AI that complements workers rather than replacing them.
3. **AI Redundancy Insurance Program** — a 12–24 month income-support scheme cushioning displaced workers during occupational transition.

The three options are recommended as an **integrated package**, phased over a 36-month implementation horizon.
